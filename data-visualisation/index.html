Comparability matters

These visualisations compare recorded hospitalisation counts, not population-adjusted rates. Avoid interpreting count differences as direct differences in individual risk.

Reading the measures

Hospitalisation counts describe the volume of admitted episodes, while bed days per hospitalisation describe average inpatient bed use. These figures are not population-adjusted rates and should not be interpreted as individual road injury risk. The “Other or unknown” category is retained as supplied.

Important data limitation

This dataset contains four road-user categories and 2021 counts for hospitalisations and bed days. It does not provide population denominators, annual trends, remoteness breakdowns or a non-Indigenous comparison group. These counts therefore should not be interpreted as population-adjusted risk or evidence of differences between First Nations and non-Indigenous people.

METHODOLOGY & TRANSPARENCY

Data integrity & provenance

Data source

BITRE hospitalised injury statistical workbooks, September 2023 release, based on the AIHW National Hospital Morbidity Database.

Reporting unit

A hospital separation represents an admitted patient episode ending in discharge, transfer or death.

Interpretation

Victoria (2012) and NSW (2017) introduced emergency department admission policy changes. These structural breaks should be considered when interpreting trends.
